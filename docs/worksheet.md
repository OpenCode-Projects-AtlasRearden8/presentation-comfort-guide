This is the whole of chapter 1 on a single page. Print it, fill it in one
sitting (90 minutes max), and your talk becomes a list instead of a fog.

If you get stuck on a line, put your best rough answer and keep moving. The
worksheet is a draft you will rehearse against, not a final engraved plaque.
